## Direction contract

THESIS: Every program this money could fund, pulled as the public-works poster it deserves. Refuses the white-page newspaper explainer with serif headlines and tidy annotations.

OWN-WORLD: Flat silkscreen inks on buff poster stock: Musk orange, 400 blue, near-black key ink, one reserved gold for the current stop and the slice being taken. Condensed poster capitals (Big Shoulders Display) for headings, stencil only on the title and the FUNDED stamp, Public Sans for reading. Funded programs are knocked out of the blue as bare stock and carry a FUNDED cancellation stamp. One printed dateline (value, source, date) per figure. Back/Next are printed ink tabs.

STORY: The reader laughs at the Musk jokes, feels the size of each program as they scroll through it, watches the pie lose slices, then learns why none of it happens, and leaves believing the gap is a political choice.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A two-ink poster: WEALTH SHOWN TO SCALE in stencil capitals at display scale, the lone $1,000 pixel on bare stock beside it, the fork credit as a printer's line, and Next as a printed tab bottom center.

FORM: Public Works Silkscreen, position 6 of the ordered grounded list (WPA Federal Art Project posters). seed:8b30b7b497903a58. Signature interaction: the knock-out, where scrolling through a program bar removes its ink and the strip's pie loses the same slice in step. Raises: datelines (ice press), state as marks (cutting bench), one oversized number per beat (shader portal), reserved highlight ink (moon bazaar), visible record of what was taken (coiled tower).
